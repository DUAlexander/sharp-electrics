About Dan Sharp, your local electrician

I've worked as an electrician for over 18 years and set up Sharp Electrics in 2024 to look after homes and businesses across the Dales and the Lune Valley.

Call 015242 33305Get a free quote

Who you'll be dealing with

When you call Sharp Electrics, you get me. I quote the job, do the work and sign off the certificates, so there's no passing you between people and nothing gets lost along the way.

My background

I spent 12 years with a firm in Settle that did the full range of electrical work: domestic, commercial, agricultural and refrigeration. That gave me experience across all kinds of buildings and equipment. In 2024 I set up Sharp Electrics so I could look after customers directly.

Registered and insured

  • NAPIT registered. This means I can certify notifiable work such as consumer unit changes and rewires, and notify building control for you.
  • TrustMark registered. TrustMark is the Government-endorsed quality scheme for trades.
  • Fully insured.
  • Current regulations. All my work is tested and certified to BS 7671, the wiring regulations.

What you can expect

  • I answer the phone. Good customer service matters to me, and you'll always get a call back.
  • Reliable. I turn up when I say I will.
  • A 12-month guarantee. My work, and any equipment I fit, is guaranteed for 12 months.
  • Part P and Building Regulations certificates where your job needs them.
  • DBS checked, so I can work in schools, care homes and similar settings.

How I work

  • Straight quotes. A clear, free quote before any work starts, with no surprises.
  • The right paperwork. You get the certificates you need, explained in plain English.
  • Respect for your home. I look after your property and leave it as I found it, as far as the job allows.

Where I work

Sharp Electrics is based in Bentham and covers the Dales and the Lune Valley: Bentham, Ingleton, Settle, Kirkby Lonsdale, Carnforth, Caton, Hornby, Milnthorpe, Long Preston and Hellifield, and the villages in between. For the right job I will also travel further, to Lancaster, Kendal and Skipton.
